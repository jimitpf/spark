About the Residency

Where sacred stone meets the creative spirit

SPARK Art Residency inhabits a 15,000-square-foot stone church built in 1903 on Eastern Avenue in Schenectady — the city the Mohawk people called Skahnéhtati, meaning "beyond the pines."

This is a city that gave the world electric light, broadcast the first television signal, and built engines that moved a nation. Now, within vaulted ceilings and gothic arches that have stood for over a century, a new kind of energy is taking shape.

SPARK offers artists the rare gift of monumental space — soaring stone halls where scale is not a limitation but an invitation. We believe transformative art requires transformative environments, and few spaces carry the weight and wonder of a century-old church reborn.

The Founder

Gunjan Tyagi
Visual Artist & Founding Director

View Her Work Instagram

Gunjan grew up in North India in a family with no connection to the arts — no artists in her family, no artists in her neighborhood. But through her father's unwavering persistence and her own determination, she forged a path that no one around her had walked before.

She earned her MFA from the prestigious Sir J.J. School of Arts in Mumbai and her BFA from Kurukshetra University. Today, she is a multimedia visual artist whose work spans painting, sculpture, installation, and film — exhibited across Hungary, Japan, South Korea, Iceland, France, Italy, Lithuania, Mauritius, Nepal, and the United States.

Having participated in residencies around the world — from Tsukuba, Japan to Wongol, South Korea to the Global Nomadic Art Project across Europe — Gunjan knows firsthand how a residency can transform an artist's trajectory. SPARK is born from that lived experience and a deeply personal mission: to give other artists the opportunity, space, and connections that can change everything.

Her vision for SPARK extends beyond studio walls — connecting resident artists with galleries, collectors, and exhibits across the East Coast, from New York City and Boston to Albany and beyond, while also opening doors for local Schenectady artists who may be just beginning their own creative journey.

Board of Directors

Rodney Dickson

Board Member

Born in Northern Ireland in 1956, Rodney Dickson is a Brooklyn-based abstract painter whose work explores the human condition through substantive layering and rich color. Shaped by the civil conflict of his formative years, his paintings challenge viewers to ponder the shared human experience. He graduated from Liverpool College of Art and moved to Brooklyn in 1997.

Rodney is the recipient of two Pollock-Krasner Foundation Awards, a Ford Foundation Artist Grant, and a John Moores Exhibition prize. His work is held by the Hugh Lane Gallery in Dublin, the Ulster Museum, the Arts Council of Great Britain, the Palmer Museum of Art at Penn State, and the Haeinsa Temple Museum in South Korea, among others. He has exhibited globally and was honored with the Belfast Ambassador Medal in 2024.

Anindita Dutta

Board Member

Anindita Dutta is a multidisciplinary artist working across performance, sculpture, installation, wet clay wall relief, and video. Her work reflects a philosophy that questions truth and pursues freedom — exploring the transient nature of what is seen, heard, and learned.

Her work has been exhibited at the Sean Kelly Gallery in New York, Fukuoka Asian Art Museum in Japan, and the Arthur M. Sackler Museum in Beijing. She is the recipient of the Pollock-Krasner Foundation Grant, a fellowship at Skowhegan School of Painting and Sculpture, the NXTHVN Fellowship, the UNESCO-Aschberg Bursaries Fellowship, and the Fukuoka Asian Art Museum Residency Grant. Her works are held in collections across the US, Japan, and China.

Our Mission

Art changed one life.
Now it opens doors for many.

SPARK exists because we believe that where you come from should never limit where your art can take you. Our founder's journey — from a small town in India with no arts community to galleries across four continents — is proof that talent, when given opportunity, knows no boundaries.

We are building a residency that serves artists at every stage: international creators seeking a powerful new environment, and local artists in Schenectady who may have never had access to professional studio space, mentorship, or the wider art world.

SPARK will connect resident artists with galleries, collectors, curators, and exhibition opportunities across the East Coast — in New York City, Boston, Albany, and the thriving arts scene of the Capital Region. We envision a future where SPARK becomes a destination — a place where renowned artists and collectors come to discover, create, and invest in the next generation of voices.

This is more than a residency. It's a launchpad, a community, and a statement: that great art can emerge from anywhere, and that a 120-year-old stone church in the Electric City is exactly where the next spark will ignite.

"Talent doesn't require a pedigree.
It requires a spark."
